# Content-Collaboration Contract

`content-collaboration-case@1.0.0` preserves audience goals, reader questions, information priority, destination, decisions, attribution, validation, unresolved choices, and next action across documentation, coauthoring, and visual critique.

Give every content item and decision a stable identity. Primary content must remain visible; secondary and reference content may move only with traceable destinations. Attribution distinguishes human instructions and accepted edits from agent proposals and source-derived material.

A complete case requires at least one audience and content item, no blocking unresolved choice, every validation gate passed or explicitly not applicable, and every source item referenced by a decision or explicitly retained. Extensions may add documentation opportunities, thread states, and visual variants without redefining shared hierarchy or authorship semantics.
