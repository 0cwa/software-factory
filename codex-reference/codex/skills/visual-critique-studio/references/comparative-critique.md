# Comparative Critique

Freeze criteria before rendering. Score or describe meaning, hierarchy, legibility, accessibility, theme behavior, responsive behavior, motion clarity, technical fit, and the user’s stated taste.

Each direction needs a distinct hypothesis and realistic context. Critique variants side by side under consistent conditions. Record observed evidence separately from preference. Select one coherent direction, or state that none passes. After refinement, rerender the chosen direction and inspect surrounding content and behavior for regression.
