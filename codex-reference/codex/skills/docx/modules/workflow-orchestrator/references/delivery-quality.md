# DOCX Delivery Quality

Treat the final DOCX as a rendered, editable package—not merely a ZIP that opens.

Before handoff, verify package integrity, required content, style and hierarchy lint, critic pass, and a rendered visual inspection of representative and high-risk pages. Inspect page breaks, headers and footers, tables, images, fields, lists, widows and orphans, clipping, overflow, contrast, and accessibility-sensitive structure. Exercise edits or field updates when editability is a requirement.

Record the exact final artifact digest and distinguish the final DOCX from previews and intermediate renders. Reject delivery when the document cannot be rendered for inspection, when lint warnings remain undispositioned, when critic status is not `pass`, when required facts or compliance inputs are unresolved, or when the registered final artifact differs from the reviewed artifact.

Loop only to the smallest responsible stage: research/compliance for facts, planner for structure, stylist for tokens, renderer for implementation, editor for targeted corrections, composer for merge defects, linter for rule failures, and critic for visual or document-fit concerns.
